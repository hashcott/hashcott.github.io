type: update

[Ghostline](https://github.com/hashcott/ghostline) 0.7.0 is out. Highlights:

- **One-click updates (Windows installer).** When a new version is available, click **update to vX** on the main screen, in Settings or in the system tray. Ghostline downloads the installer, checks it against the release's signed `SHA256SUMS`, installs over the current version and reopens, reconnecting if you were connected. No uninstall needed, and your settings are kept.
- **Coming from 0.6.3 or older:** run the new installer over your current install once. From 0.7.0 on, the update button does this for you.
- **Beta builds.** Turn on *Receive beta versions* in Settings to get pre-release builds earlier (off by default).
- **Download page:** [hashcott.github.io/ghostline](https://hashcott.github.io/ghostline/) picks the right file for your machine, with a guide, FAQ and changelog.

Full details are in the [release notes](https://github.com/hashcott/ghostline/releases/tag/v0.7.0).
