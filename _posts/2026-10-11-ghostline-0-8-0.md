[Ghostline](https://github.com/hashcott/ghostline) 0.8.0 is out. Highlights:

- **Terms of use, accepted once.** The first time you open 0.8.0 (also right after updating), Ghostline shows its terms of use instead of the interface. Read them to the end, tick the box and choose *agree and continue*, or *disagree, quit*. Your answer stays on your computer and is not copied by settings backups.
- **What Ghostline is for.** The app, the download page and the guides now describe it plainly: encrypted DNS your network cannot read or spoof, and protection against DPI interference with connections to lawful sites. A few labels changed to match, for example *auto when blocked* is now *auto on interference*.
- **Use it within the law.** The terms and the [disclaimer](https://github.com/hashcott/ghostline#disclaimer) ask you to follow the law where you live. That includes not reaching sites blocked under the law, and not showing others how to.
- **Updating:** from 0.7.0 on, click **update to v0.8.0** in the app. Otherwise get it from the [download page](https://hashcott.github.io/ghostline/).

Full details are in the [release notes](https://github.com/hashcott/ghostline/releases/tag/v0.8.0).
